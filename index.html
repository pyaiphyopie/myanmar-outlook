FIELD BRIEF // OCTOBER 2026

Myanmar: The Next Five Years

A neutral assessment of where the country stands today and the two paths it could take by 2031.

Prepared for internal review · Not for public distribution
01

The Present

Political Reality

Since the February 2021 coup, the country has been governed by the State Administration Council. Armed resistance has expanded across multiple states and regions. No national election has been held under the current authorities. International recognition remains limited to a handful of states.

Security Landscape

Conflict is no longer confined to border areas. Urban resistance, drone warfare, and sabotage have reached Yangon and Mandalay. Daily life in many townships involves checkpoints, curfews, and intermittent connectivity.

Humanitarian Strain

Over three million people have been displaced internally. Food insecurity affects millions more. Health systems are degraded; maternal and child mortality indicators have worsened. Aid access is constrained by both conflict and administrative barriers.

02

Economy

Currency & Inflation

The kyat has lost the majority of its value against the dollar since 2021. Inflation remains elevated, driven by import costs, fuel prices, and supply disruption. Parallel market exchange rates diverge sharply from official rates.

Banking & Reserves

Foreign exchange reserves have been drawn down significantly. Cross-border trade has shifted toward barter and informal channels. Formal banking penetration remains low, which is precisely why mobile wallets like KBZPay and WavePay matter so much.

Sector Shifts

Garment exports have partially recovered but remain below pre-coup levels. Agriculture still employs the majority of the workforce. The digital economy — fintech, e-commerce, content creation — is one of the few growth areas, constrained by payment rails and connectivity.

03

Digital

Connectivity

Mobile penetration is high but data costs are among the region's most expensive relative to income. Internet shutdowns and throttling have been used as control tools. Offline-first design is not optional — it is the baseline.

Local Platforms

KBZPay and WavePay dominate payments. Facebook remains the primary information channel despite blocks. TikTok has faced restrictions since late 2025. Local language AI tools are nearly nonexistent — which is the gap this portfolio exists to fill.

The Voice Gap

Burmese TTS and STT quality lags far behind English and major Asian languages. Existing solutions are either generic clones or English-first products with Burmese bolted on. Consent-based, community-rooted voice infrastructure does not yet exist at scale.

04

Society

Migration & Brain Drain

Hundreds of thousands have left since 2021 — professionals, students, and skilled workers. Thailand, Malaysia, Japan, and Singapore absorb the largest flows. The diaspora is a potential resource if re-engagement channels exist.

Education Disruption

University classes have been suspended and resumed in cycles. Many students study online or abroad. The long-term cost to human capital is compounding.

Civil Society

Despite repression, mutual aid networks, community schools, and local media persist. Trust in institutions is low; trust in community is high. Any rebuild starts from the latter.

05

The 2031 Horizon

2027

Stabilization phase. Conflict intensity may decrease in some regions as resources exhaust, but no political settlement is likely. Digital infrastructure quietly matures underground.

2028–2029

Digital-layer maturity. Mobile wallets, local AI, and offline tools reach critical mass. A generation raised on crisis tech becomes the workforce.

2030–2031

The fork. Path A: negotiated reopening, sanctions ease, capital returns. Path B: prolonged re-isolation, parallel economy deepens, the diaspora becomes permanent. Which path depends on factors no model can predict.

The honest read: Myanmar's next five years will be defined less by grand political events and more by whether ordinary people can build, transact, and communicate when the system fails them. That is not a prediction. It is the design brief.
